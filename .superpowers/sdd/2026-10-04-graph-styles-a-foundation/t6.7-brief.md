## Goal
A figure's SVG holds `<pattern data-paper-key="KEY">` with `<image data-paper-key="KEY" href="">` inside. The key is self-describing: `paper-v1:<type>:<seed>:<size>:<texture>:<base hex>`, and `parsePaperKey` in `style/papers/generated.ts` reads it. host.ts fills those hrefs in a browser.
