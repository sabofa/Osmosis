## Important
1. style/papers/rulings.ts:48-55 (graph) — the lines at k=0 are drawn on the pattern tile's own edge (x=0 / y=0) and the tile has no line at k=SQUARES. SVG patterns clip to the tile, so each tile keeps only the inner half of that line and its neighbour never draws the other half. Failure: every tenth graph line, in both directions (graph and rough-graph, and the strong line at k=0), renders at half width, a regular visible stripe across the sheet. Fix: also draw each k=0 line at `tile` (or offset the pattern by half a stroke) so the clipped halves meet; add a test that counts a line at 0 and at tile. Notebook (rules at (k+1)g-0.5) and dotted (shifted -g/2) are not affected.

## Minor
2. style/papers/host.ts:57-69 — `blobUrls` caches a rejected promise forever. If `generatePaper` throws (a tile outside 16..4096 from a hand-built key, a canvas failure) every later `fillPaperTiles` call re-awaits the rejection and aborts the loop, so the remaining images on the page stay empty; the other keys' promises become unhandled rejections. Failure: one bad key blanks all papers on the page. Catch inside `blobUrlFor` and resolve null.
