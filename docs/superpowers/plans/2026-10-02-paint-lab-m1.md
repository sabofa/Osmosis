### Task 2: The paint model (branch `milestone-a/paint-model`)

**Interfaces.** Consumes the contract. Produces the following in `graph-engine/src/space/paint/model/index.ts`:
```ts
export const buildParticles: BuildParticles
export const paintFrame: PaintFrameFn
```
It also produces `model/testing.ts`, with `sphereGBuffer(width, height, opts)` and `planeGBuffer(...)` for the tests of Tasks 2, 3 and 6.

**Decisions.** Port these from the mockup, using the spec's numbers, which are the defaults in params.ts:
- **M1. Particles (§3.1).**
  - Area-weighted blue noise per mesh: sample triangles by area with seeded barycentrics, then thin by a Poisson-disc grid at radius `1/√maxPerUnit2`.
  - Seed from `randomFor('paint/particles/' + markIndex, params.seed)`.
  - The tangent comes from the uv gradient (∂P/∂u, choosing whichever of u or v crosses the light direction more, as in the mockup). Without uv, use the direction of greatest normal change.
  - The cell is a hash of `floor(position / mix.loadCell)`.
  - The local colour comes from `SceneColours`. For a colour scale, use the mesh's `scalars` interpolated at the particle.
- **M2. View (§3.2).**
  - A particle is visible when its projected depth is within ε of the G-buffer depth at its pixel.
  - Draw it when `rank < clamp(targetPer10kPx / 10000 · pxArea(particle), 0, 1)`. Here `pxArea` is the screen area the particle's share of surface covers (world area per particle × px per world unit² × |n·v|). Multiply by `particles.dragDensity` while dragging.
  - Fade alpha over |n·v| ∈ [fadeLo, fadeHi].
- **M3. Value plan (§3.3):** zones from u with soft boundaries, plateaus, and a seeded smooth deviation.
- **M4. Curve (§3.4):** exactly the formulas in the spec, in OKLCH, with tints added in OKLab. Colormapped particles scale hue and tints by `colormapHue`. L is never touched by tints.
- **M5. Mix (§3.5):**
  - Loads are consecutive strokes of one role in layer order. A load breaks after `loadMin..loadMax` strokes (seeded) or a jump of more than `loadBreakPx`.
  - The offset is seeded per load from `randomFor('paint/load/' + role + '/' + cell, seed)`.
  - Anti-correlation, drift, the grey vectors, value hold and value steps follow the spec.
  - Per-role and colormap scales apply.
  - Value hold: after applying the hue and chroma offset, re-solve L so the OKLab L stays within ±`valueHold` of the target, before the optional value step.
- **M6. Planes and edges (§3.6):**
  - Planes are connected components of (quantised normal cell × zone) on the G-buffer. Merge pieces under `planeMinPx` (in CSS px², scaled by the G-buffer scale) into the neighbour they share the longest border with.
  - Edge hardness is computed per boundary sample and smoothed by a median of 7. Classes come from the spec's thresholds.
  - Each stroke's behaviour comes from the class of its nearest edge with contrast ≥ 0.05 within 20 px, else from `0.52 + 0.26·light − 0.28·shadow`.
  - Strokes stop at, bleed through, or run past plane edges by `stopAt` and `bleedAt`.
- **M7. Contours (§3.6):**
  - Zero crossings of n·v on the mesh triangles, chained into polylines and segmented by a hash of surface position.
  - Each segment is classed by its contrast against the canvas tone.
  - They become 'edge' strokes.
- **M8. Roles (§3.7):**
  - **Block** is on every visible particle (subsampled by role density).
  - **Form** goes on particles in the light and half-tone zones near the terminator, oriented along the tangent and stopping below the core.
  - **Scumble** goes where the transition zone is wide (the gradient of u in the G-buffer is below a threshold over at least 6 px), alternating light and dark by parity.
  - **Glaze** goes in the core and cast zones, and on meshes with opacity below 1, where it is the only role, at alpha 0.26.
  - **Reflected** goes in the reflected zone.
  - **Dab** goes on the top `roles.dab.density` fraction of u maxima, at least 12 px apart.
  - Each role's width, length, curvature, load, impasto, bristles, dry and wet come from `params.roles[role]`, with a slight seeded per-instance variation (±12%).
- **M9. Strokes:**
  - A stroke's path is walked on the surface along the tangent for `length` CSS px, projected, curved by `curvature`, and resampled to `PATH_POINTS` points.
  - Width is shaped by pressure: a loaded start, then a taper over the last 30%.
  - Colour is the curve plus the mix, converted to linear sRGB.
  - Depth is the view depth at the start.
- **M10. Lines (§3.7.8):**
  - LineMarks (curves, axes, box), ArrowMarks and PointMarks become 'line' strokes along their exact projected geometry.
  - Their colour is the mark's colour shifted by the curve at u = 0.6 plus the mix ×`roleLine`, as the mockup's umber is.
  - Points are a short loaded dab. Arrowheads are two short strokes meeting exactly at the tip.
  - No position perturbation, ever.

**Tests** (`model/*.test.ts`, hand values; each test must fail when its behaviour is deleted):
- **colour.ts:** an OKLab round trip, and known sRGB↔OKLab pairs (white, mid grey 0.5 → L 0.7016).
- **curve.ts** at u ∈ {0.24, 0.5, 0.62, 0.9} for a terracotta local colour (L 0.56, C 0.14, h 38°), hand-computed from the spec formulas. On a colormapped colour, the hue rotation is a third.
- **mix.ts:**
  - every mixed colour keeps |ΔL| ≤ 0.012 except value-step loads, which stay ≤ 0.012 + 0.03;
  - over 2,000 loads, about 25% are value-step loads (±3%);
  - neighbouring hue flip frequency is 0.8 ± 0.03;
  - the drift ratio on the last stroke of a load is 0.45;
  - a grey colour (C < greyChroma) gets an a/b offset of 0.012–0.026.
- **value.ts:** zone boundaries, and the plateaus.
- **edges.ts:** constructed G-buffers. A sharp normal crease with high contrast is HARD. A smooth sphere terminator is SOFT. Two adjacent planes of equal value are LOST. A cast shadow is harder near the occluder than far from it.
- **planes.ts:** a sphere G-buffer gives more than 20 planes and no plane under `planeMinPx`.
- **view.ts:** screen density is within ±15% of target at two zooms. Retention of the same particles between views 12° apart is at least 0.8 (sphere).
- **index.ts:** determinism (the same inputs give a byte-identical StrokeBatch). Data marks: a 'line' stroke's path points lie on the projected polyline within 0.01 px. Colormapped surfaces stay within ×1/3 hue.

### Task 3: The paint renderer (branch `milestone-a/paint-gl`)

**Interfaces.** Consumes the contract. Produces `graph-engine/src/space/paint/gl/PaintRenderer.ts`:
```ts
export class PaintRenderer {
  constructor(canvas: HTMLCanvasElement)            // WebGL2 or throws a shown error the lab displays
  setScene(scene: SpaceScene, colours: SceneColours): void   // uploads meshes (positions, normals, mark index)
  setPaper(rgba: Uint8ClampedArray, height: Float32Array, size: number): void
  renderGBuffer(view: PaintView, params: PaintParams): GBuffer  // G-buffer + shadow map, readback at scale 2
  paint(frame: PaintFrame, view: PaintView, params: PaintParams, debug: PaintDebugMode): void
  dispose(): void                                    // frees every GL object, loseContext
}
```

**Decisions:**
- **R1. G-buffer pass.**
  - Render to MRT. Use RGBA16F when `EXT_color_buffer_float` is available, otherwise RGBA8 with an encode (store normal as n·0.5+0.5, value in a channel, mark index in two channels, depth packed).
  - It holds view depth, world normal, raw lit value u (§3.3: key Lambert × intensity × shadow, plus ambient, plus sky·max(n.z, 0), plus bounce·max(−n.z, 0), clamped to 0..1), the shadow flag and the mark index.
  - Read it back with one `readPixels` per attachment into the `GBuffer` at scale 2 (half the CSS resolution).
- **R2. Shadow map.** Depth from the key light, orthographic, fitted to the scene's bounding sphere, at 1024², with a slope-scaled bias. The G-buffer pass samples it with a 3×3 PCF.
- **R3. Stroke passes.**
  - One pass per `LAYER_ORDER` layer, back to front by `depth` within the layer (sort on the CPU).
  - Each stroke is a ribbon of `PATH_POINTS − 1` quads: instanced, or a dynamic vertex buffer.
  - The fragment shader is a procedural brush, ported from the mockup's painter.js:
    - bristle ridges across the ribbon (count `bristles`, per-bristle load from seeded 1D noise, variance `bristleVar`);
    - a loaded start, and a dry tail over the last `dry` fraction, gated by the canvas height texture so paint skips the weave valleys;
    - `endSoft` dissolving the end.
  - Output goes to an accumulation framebuffer: premultiplied colour, plus paint height and coverage in a second attachment.
  - Wet pickup: the shader samples the previous layers' colour texture at the fragment and mixes it by `wet`. Ping-pong two colour targets between layers.
- **R4. Composite.**
  - The canvas comes from `setPaper`, tiled in screen space at 1:1 device px (it never moves with the camera).
  - Paint goes over by coverage.
  - Relief: normals from the gradient of (paint height × `impasto.strength` + canvas height × `canvas.texture` × 0.3), lit at (`impasto.lightAzimuth`, `impasto.lightElevation`), applied as a gentle multiply and sheen.
  - The result goes to the default framebuffer, 4× MSAA not required.
- **R5. Debug modes** (`PaintDebugMode`):
  - `value`: the G-buffer u in grey;
  - `zones` and `planes`: `frame.debug.zones` and `frame.debug.planes` as flat colours;
  - `edges`: segments in lost grey, soft blue, firm orange, hard red over a faint grey plane tint;
  - `roles`: each stroke flat-coloured by role;
  - `grey`: the final image converted to OKLab L;
  - `paint-only`: no canvas.
- **R6. Lifecycle.** Context loss shows a message and recovers on restore. `dispose` frees everything. Resizing follows the canvas client size × devicePixelRatio.
- **R7. Performance.** At 1280×800 with 15k strokes, aim for 30 fps on the review machine. Measure and report it. Batch all strokes of a layer in one draw.

**Tests** (fake GL, following `graph-engine/src/space/gl/fakeGl.ts`):
- pass order: shadow, then G-buffer, then readback, then the layers in `LAYER_ORDER`, then composite;
- one draw call per non-empty layer;
- ping-pong alternates;
- `dispose` deletes every created object;
- the readback packs a known 2×2 G-buffer into the `GBuffer` arrays correctly. Unit-test the RGBA8 encode/decode both ways.

**Decisions:**
